Aurèle Vasseur, freelance interactive developer. I build WebGL, shader-driven and real-time experiences for studios and brands, and I care about the quiet details: how light moves through a surface, how a page breathes when you stop touching it.

Since 2016 I have worked with Studio Marée, Ondine, Atelier Nord and independent artists, on everything from launch sites to museum installations. I also sit on the Awwwards jury.

Based in Lyon, available for new collaborations from spring 2026. The stone on the right is lit from inside: each project changes its minerals.
