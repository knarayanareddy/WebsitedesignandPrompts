3. **WebGL & Canvas Stage**:
   - Render a fixed full-viewport `<canvas>` (`devicePixelRatio` capped at `2.0`) implementing the core visual effect: A single screen of blue meadow you can comb with your cursor, a floating television lighting the grass from the crest, cyan motes rising through a cumulus sky, and a viewfinder frame of frosted pills and hairlines that blur in around a script headline once a counting curtain lifts.
   - Bind normalized scroll progress `uScroll` (`0.0 -> 1.0`) and spring-smoothed pointer position `uPointer` (`[-1, 1]`) as shader/scene uniforms.
