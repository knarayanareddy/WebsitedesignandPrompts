## Quick tips for using these as website backgrounds

```html
<video class="bg-video" autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="metadata"
       poster="poster.jpg">
  <source src="snowboard-1080p.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>
```

- **File size matters more than resolution.** The 1080p links above are 4–36 MB; for a hero background aim for ≤ ~15 MB. If a clip is too long (27–51 s), trim it to 6–12 s and re-encode:
  `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -t 10 -c:v libx264 -crf 23 -preset slow -an -movflags +faststart out.mp4`
- **`muted` + `playsinline`** are required for autoplay in Safari/iOS.
- Add a **dark overlay** (`linear-gradient` or `backdrop-filter`) for text contrast — especially helpful on the sunny clips.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (pause the video or show the poster).
- All clips here are 16:9 — fine with `object-fit: cover` on full-bleed sections.
