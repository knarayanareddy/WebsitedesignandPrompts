Apple — Style Reference

A gallery wall lit by daylight — oversized type drops shadows on a white surface, and color enters the frame only through the product itself.

Theme: light

The MacBook Neo product page reads like a cool, luminous gallery space — a near-white canvas where weight-700 headlines at 80–96px sit above feather-light body copy, holding a deliberate tension between mass and air. The page background never quite reaches pure white, settling at #f5f5f7 so cards and content wells lift off the surface without needing a single shadow. Letter-spacing tightens as type scales up — display headlines track at -0.022em while body copy breathes at -0.003em — a signature move that makes large type feel chiseled instead of slack.
