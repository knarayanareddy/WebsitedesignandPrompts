# Adapted Prompt Kit: Yogi Suria — Neoclassical Digital Portfolio for Web3 & AI

Use this prompt specification to generate a high-impact, brutalist-neoclassical portfolio or agency website inspired by **Yogi Suria** (`Stoik Agency®`):

---

## Prompt Specification

```markdown
Build a bold neoclassical brutalist portfolio website for a design partner catering to Web3 and AI founders:

1. Visual Direction & Atmosphere:
   - Palette: Muted neutral stone canvas (#E5E5E5 / #ECECEC) with subtle vertical 6-column architectural grid guide lines (#D4D4D4).
   - Core Contrast: Deep ink blacks (#0A0A0A) for typography and card elements with vibrant electric blue accents (#0066FF / #0099FF) on primary CTA buttons.
   - Neoclassical Tech Juxtaposition: Classical Greco-Roman marble statues blended seamlessly with modern technology (marble philosopher wearing silver over-ear headphones, holding an iPhone and a MacBook Pro).

2. Typography:
   - Display Headlines: Heavy neo-grotesque sans-serif (Geist / Inter Display, font-weight: 700-800) with tight tracking (-0.03em) and generous line height (1.1).
   - Accent Pixel Fonts: Monospace pixelated serif or bitmap display typeface (FK Raster Sharp) for stamp badges, preloader counters, and section markers.
   - Body & Meta: Clean neutral sans-serif (Geist / Inter) for narrative descriptions, approach comparisons, and legal footers.

3. Hero & 3D Interactive Centerpiece:
   - Spline 3D Scene: Embedded 3D model of the headphone-wearing philosopher responding subtly to mouse movement with ambient lighting and metallic reflections.
   - Dual-Row Looping Project Reels: Infinite horizontal carousels displaying alternating 16:9 mockup cards with auto-playing looping video clips (WebM/MP4) and product interfaces.
   - Live Social Proof Ticker: Floating verification pill bar showcasing real-time endorsements and testimonials from industry figures with circular avatars and verified badges.

4. Structural Sections:
   - Solutions Taxonomy: Minimalist typographic list of design services (Brand Identity, Product Design, Web Development, Motion & Animation, Design Systems, Prototyping) flanking dark floating glass project cards.
   - Two Approaches Comparative Cards:
     - Approach 1 (Traditional): Clean white card describing orthodox process (discovery, scoping, sprints, reviews) paired with a classical engraved etching of the philosopher sitting cross-legged with a laptop.
     - Approach 2 (Just Bring It): Parallel white card describing rapid high-cadence execution paired with an engraved etching of the philosopher doing one-arm pushups over a laptop.
   - Skybox Contact Banner:
     - Panoramic idyllic blue sky with puffy cumulus clouds framing a reclining classical marble statue, accompanied by the bold headline "Have a brief? Let's ship it." and an electric blue mail button.

5. Technical Constraints:
   - 100% self-contained local assets (WOFF2 fonts, OTF glyphs, WebM video reels, 3D Spline scene binaries).
   - Zero external third-party telemetry, tracking, or blocking CDN calls.
   - Offline Draco WebAssembly decompression for instant 3D model loading.
```
