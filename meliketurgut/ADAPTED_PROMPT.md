# Adapted Prompt Kit: Melike Turgut — Tactile Gummy Portfolio & Editorial Storytelling

Use this prompt specification to generate a tactile, playful, and editorial personal portfolio or case study gallery inspired by **Melike Turgut**:

---

## Prompt Specification

```markdown
Create an interactive, tactile portfolio website celebrating career milestones through playful 3D physics charms and Swiss editorial typography:

1. Color Palette & Canvas Atmosphere:
   - Primary Background: Soft, warm sage/oatmeal paper tone (`#eaece5`).
   - Primary Text: Deep olive/forest ink (`#40400c`) and crisp dark olive (`#111827`).
   - Accents: Candy-specific bright glossy hues (candy-apple red, luminous lemon, lilac, royal plum, electric cyan, tangerine, opal frost, emerald green).
   - Card/Sheet Paper: Pure ivory/eggshell (`#FFFFFF` / `#FAF9F6`) with soft rounded corners and deep layered drop shadows.

2. Typographic System:
   - Hero Branding: Distinctive wide geometric sans-serif (Pangram Pangram Agrandir or Syne), creating a dramatic typographical bracket: `me` [charms] `like`.
   - Editorial Sheet Numerals: Ultra-light, high-contrast display serif (PP Editorial New Ultralight or Playfair Display Hairline) for giant dates (`2011—12`, `2020—23`).
   - Story Body: Neutral grotesque sans-serif with comfortable line height (1.6) and generous measure.
   - Hand-Drawn Notes: Cursive ballpoint handwriting (Belmonte Ballpoint or Reenie Beanie) for chapter signatures, secret x-ray annotations, and margin remarks.

3. Tactile 3D Gummy Charm System:
   - 8 unique sculpted gummy objects arranged in a horizontal hero row.
   - Realistic candy material properties: translucent subsurface scattering, specular gloss highlights, refractive edges, and squishy physics.
   - Physics & Motion: Interactive pull/drag inertia with spring-damping restitution, tilt angle on cursor move, and squash-and-stretch upon landing.
   - Multi-resolution responsive asset scaling based on DPR and container width.

4. Dual-State Editorial Story Sheet (World Dialog):
   - Clicking a charm opens a split-screen narrative world:
     - Left Stage: Enlarged 3D charm suspended in space with handwritten company moniker and navigation arrows to neighboring chapters.
     - Right Sheet: Floating editorial card with giant date header, structured premises/realities summary table ("The Premise" vs "The Reality"), and long-form narrative.
   - The Plot Slider: An interactive scrubber bar with a miniature gummy thumb that expands/reveals hidden paragraphs and behind-the-scenes case-study images as it is dragged.
   - High-fidelity case study media: Embedded looping borderless MP4 video reels and responsive webp screenshots.

5. Accessibility & Performance:
   - 100% self-contained local assets (WOFF2 fonts, WebP renders, local JSON fixtures).
   - Zero third-party telemetry, tracking, or blocking scripts.
   - Fallback SVG/CSS spring animations for devices without WebGL support.
   - Full keyboard navigation: arrow keys cycle chapters, Escape closes dialogs, Tab navigates the plot slider.
```
